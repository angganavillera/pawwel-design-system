Motion System

PAWWEL's motion is physics-based, never linear. Every animation should feel like something that has weight and life — especially the robot.

Duration Scale

duration.instant100msHaptic feedback confirmation, dot state
duration.fast150msButton press, pill color change
duration.normal250msCard hover, tab switch, icon change
duration.medium350msSheet presentation, card expand
duration.slow500msScreen transitions, robot state change
duration.crawl800msScore reveal, emotional arc animation
duration.idle-loop1600msRobot breathing cycle (repeat)

Spring Presets

spring.snappyresponse:0.25Button press feedback, quick state
spring.defaultresponse:0.35Tab switch, card interactions
spring.gentleresponse:0.5Sheet presentation, robot entrance
spring.bouncyresponse:0.4, damp:0.6Robot Playful state, treat dispense
spring.idleresponse:1.6, damp:0.7Robot breathing idle loop

Robot Animation Behaviors

State Animation Spring Loop
Calm & Happy Scale 1.0→1.02→1.0 (breathing) spring.idle Yes · 1.6s
Playful Bounce Y +4pt, head tilt ±5° spring.bouncy Yes · 0.8s
Slightly Uneasy Small gaze shifts, slower idle spring.gentle Yes · 1.2s
Needs Attention Attentive posture, soft alert pulse spring.default Yes · 0.8s
Overstimulated Fast breathing, restrained shake spring.snappy Yes · 0.4s
Resting Slow head droop, eyes close spring.idle Yes · 3s
🎁 Treat dispense Jump +12pt, spin, return spring.bouncy No · once
🎵 Music trigger Sway ±8°, ears wiggle spring.gentle Yes · 2s
😴 Sleep Slow head droop, eyes close spring.idle Yes · 3s

Motion Principles

  • Motion has weight. The robot breathes, the cards settle, the sheets fall into place. Nothing snaps into existence.
  • Motion reinforces, never announces. If the user notices the animation more than the change, the motion is too loud.
  • Looped motion stays calm. Idle and breathing loops use long springs so the eye reads life, not activity.
  • One-shot motion has personality. Treat dispense, outfit changes, and celebrations are allowed to be playful — but always brief.
  • Reduce Motion is honored everywhere. All idle loops, transitions, and decorative motion collapse to an instant change when the system requests it.

iOS-Native Rules

PAWWEL is iOS-first. These rules exist so the app feels like part of the system, not a brand placed on top of it.

Safe Area & Layout

Rule Value Why
Screen horizontal margin 20pt · spacing.2xl Matches the gutter used by iOS system apps.
Tab bar height 49pt + safe area Bar always sits above the home-indicator region.
Navigation bar 44pt + status bar Inline navigation only — large titles read as enterprise in PAWWEL.
Dynamic Island 54pt top inset The top zone belongs to the system. PAWWEL never places content there.
Minimum touch target 44 × 44pt iOS HIG. Enforced on every interactive surface, regardless of visual size.
Full-bleed corner radius ~40pt Cards that reach the device edge match the physical corner.

Haptic Direction

Haptics carry emotional reinforcement, not utility. Every haptic in PAWWEL has a feeling attached.

Moment Intensity What it should feel like
Treat dispense Medium impact A small, satisfying confirmation — the moment lands.
Anxiety detected Warning notification An alert without sound — gentle but present.
Button press Light impact Micro acknowledgement — the system heard you.
Outfit changed Success notification A small celebration. Joyful, not loud.
Tab switch Selection The subtle click of choice — easily missed, never absent.

iOS

Behavior Expectations

System Setting Expectation in PAWWEL
Reduce Motion All idle loops, breathing animations, decorative transitions, and robot motion collapse to instant changes. Functional motion (sheet present/dismiss) remains, but with springs disabled.
Dynamic Type Every SF Pro text style scales with the user's preferred size up to .accessibility3 without truncation or layout breakage.
Increase Contrast Borders thicken from hairline to 1pt. Subtle surface tints shift one step toward higher contrast.
VoiceOver Every icon, image, and decorative pill exposes a meaningful label. Decorative-only elements are explicitly hidden from accessibility.
Bold Text SF Pro Regular shifts to SF Pro Medium. SF Pro Semibold stays as-is — it already reads as bold.
🤝
Accessibility is product, not polish
PAWWEL is for every pet owner. Reduced motion, larger type, VoiceOver, and high contrast are first-class states of the product — they ship with the same care as the default experience, never as an afterthought.

PAWWEL Emotional State Logic

PAWWEL combines anxiety, activity, and engagement signals into emotionally understandable state labels. Scores are interpreted as companion context, not clinical analytics.

Anxiety Activity Engagement State Pawwel Score
Low (10) Normal (50) High (80) Calm & Happy 90–100
Low (20) High (70) High (75) Playful 80–90
Medium (40) Normal (50) Medium (50) Slightly Uneasy 60–75
High (70) Low (20) Low (30) Needs Attention 30–50
High (80) High (90) Medium (50) Overstimulated 20–40
Low (15) Low (10) Low (20) Resting 65–75

Interpretation Philosophy

PAWWEL translates raw sensor data into emotionally understandable states. Interpretation is prioritized over analytics: labels should feel supportive, not clinical, and scores are contextual and non-medical.

Behavior Notes

Emotional State System

The entire app adapts to the pet's current emotional state. This is what makes PAWWEL a companion app — not a tracker.

CALM & HAPPY
Low anxiety, normal activity, and high engagement. The pet feels emotionally safe and connected.
Accent: green.60
Robot: slow breathing + soft attention
Home bg: white with supportive green tint
Quick action: "Give Treat"
PLAYFUL
High activity and strong engagement. Energetic, curious, and cheerful without feeling urgent.
Accent: yellow.60
Robot: bouncy movement + bright response
Home bg: white with warm yellow tint
Quick action: "Play Music"
SLIGHTLY UNEASY
Medium anxiety with neutral activity and engagement. Subtle discomfort, low urgency.
Accent: color.base.brown.30
Robot: gentler idle + slower gaze shifts
Home bg: warm taupe surface
Quick action: "Check In"
NEEDS ATTENTION
High anxiety with low activity and engagement. Noticeable concern with supportive urgency.
Accent: orange.60
Robot: attentive posture + soft alert
Notification: supportive check-in
Quick action: "Play Calming Music"
OVERSTIMULATED
High anxiety and high activity. Emotionally overloaded and the highest urgency state.
Accent: red.50
Robot: faster breathing + restrained motion
Notification: immediate but calm support
Quick action: "Start Calming Mode"
RESTING
Low activity and low engagement with low anxiety. Sleep, rest, or quiet low-energy time.
Accent: gray.50
Robot: eyes closed, still
Home: soft neutral state
Quick actions: all dimmed
✅
Principle: Name the feeling, don't measure it
Always display the emotional interpretation ("Apple needs attention right now") rather than the raw score. Users are pet parents, not data scientists. The number lives in the system — only the story reaches the screen.

Supportive Urgency: Needs Attention

When concern is detected, the app must communicate urgency without panic. Orange is used for supportive attention; pink-red is reserved for overstimulation.

Apple needs attention
Higher than usual concern detected. Playing calming music might help.
